Uncertainty sampling, diversity sampling, and hybrid query strategies choose unlabeled pool examples for annotation so model accuracy reaches a target under a fixed labeling budget. Offline training-loop query selection under /app/label_suites drifted after the sampler upgrade. Work only in /app/samplegate, /app/pickcore, /app/stratops, and /app/effimath. Leave /app/label_suites, /app/sampling_policy, /app/go.mod, and /app/Makefile unchanged. Treat /app/sampling_policy/active_learning_contract.json as the normative specification for keys, formulas, finding details, gate order, and label accounting. Produce /app/build/alsctl writing /app/build/active_learning_report.json for every suite in /app/label_suites/manifest.json.

SEED_LABEL must not increment labels_used or consume budget. Hybrid ties break by higher diversity then sample_id. Independent model-score channels report score_round as the min of both channel rounds. CHANNEL_SKEW fires when abs channel-round gap is strictly greater than max_channel_skew_rounds before STALE_SCORE. POST_LABEL_SCORE must not increment score_calls. ORPHAN_SCORE uses audit_seq equal to max seq plus one. Annotation efficiency multiplies accuracy gain by 100 then divides by labels_used and clamps negatives to zero. Confidence uses final times labels_used over labels_used plus confidence_z2. Defaults then top-level then policy_overrides including max_channel_skew_rounds.

Emit compact JSON with no spaces after colon or comma, empty arrays, one trailing newline, and byte-identical reruns. Sort samples by sample_id and findings by finding_id. ACCEPTED only when findings are empty. Binary only at /app/build/alsctl.
